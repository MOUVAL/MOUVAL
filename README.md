### What I do

- **Product visualisation & CAD rendering** — industrial and automotive clients, from STEP file to final frame
- **Web 3D** — Three.js / GLB configurators and interactive showrooms
- **Blender tooling** — add-ons and material libraries (MAML, EZBake, MStep) used by thousands of artists
- **Games** — [8DRIFT](https://8drift.com), a top-down arcade drift game, approved for Full Launch on CrazyGames
- **Products** — [OIO](https://oio.app), a SaaS built solo, launched on Product Hunt in 2026

Most of the day-to-day work lives in private client repos — the contribution graph is the honest picture.
